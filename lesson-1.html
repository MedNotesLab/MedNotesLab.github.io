🔬 What Is Health Science?

Health science is a broad area of science that focuses on understanding health , the human body, disease, healthcare, and the factors that influence well-being.

Health science is not one single subject. It brings together knowledge from many fields, including biology, chemistry, psychology, genetics, medicine, public health, and scientific research.

🌷 Health

A broad state of well-being involving physical, mental, and social aspects of life.

⭐ KEY IDEA

Health science is not just about doctors or hospitals. It also involves understanding how the body works, investigating disease, studying prevention, evaluating treatments, and improving our understanding of health.

🫀 Health Science & the Human Body

One major part of health science is understanding the structures that make up the human body. Scientists study cells, tissues, organs, and body systems to understand how they function together.

🌷 What Does "Health" Mean?

It is easy to think of health as simply "not being sick." However, health is broader than the absence of disease.

Health can involve physical, mental, and social aspects of well-being. Environmental and behavioral factors can also influence health.

🫀 Physical Health

Relates to the condition and functioning of the body's physical structures and systems.

🧠 Mental Health

Relates to psychological and emotional well-being, including thoughts, feelings, and behavior.

👥 Social Health

Relates to relationships, interactions with others, and social environments.

🌎 Environment

A person's surroundings and environmental conditions can influence health.

🧪 What Does "Science" Mean?

Science is a systematic way of developing knowledge about the natural world. Scientists ask questions, collect evidence, analyze information, and evaluate explanations.

🔎 A Simplified Scientific Investigation

❓ Question

What are we trying to understand?

🔬 Evidence

What information can we collect?

📊 Analysis

What does the evidence show?

📝 Conclusion

What can we reasonably conclude?

🧬 Major Areas of Health Science

Health science contains many different fields. Each field focuses on different questions about health and the body.

🦴 Anatomy

The study of body structures and how those structures are organized.

⚙️ Physiology

The study of how living organisms and their parts function.

🧬 Genetics

The study of genes, DNA, heredity, and genetic variation.

🦠 Microbiology

The study of microorganisms and their interactions with living organisms.

🧠 Psychology

The study of behavior and mental processes.

🌎 Public Health

The study and practice of protecting and improving health at the population level.

💊 Pharmacology

The study of how drugs and other substances interact with living organisms.

🔬 Medical Research

Scientific investigation into health, disease, prevention, diagnosis, and treatment.

🔗 How Do These Fields Connect?

Health problems can be complicated. A single question may require information from several scientific fields.
