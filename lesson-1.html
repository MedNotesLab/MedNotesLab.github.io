Health science is the study of the human body, health, disease, medicine, and the ways scientists work to understand and improve health.

What Is Health Science?

Health science includes many different fields that study the human body, diseases, treatments, and ways to prevent illness. It combines knowledge from biology, chemistry, medicine, psychology, and other sciences.
